# Paginary Brand Identity

## Overview
**Paginary** — A multi-model, multi-modality reasoning framework for adaptive page composition.

**Tagline:** Adaptive, intelligent page layouts.

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Accent | #84B08E | Primary interactive elements, CTAs, highlights |
| Neutral | #6B7280 | Body text, secondary UI, backgrounds |
| Background | #F5F0E8 | Warm, paper-like canvas for content |

## Typography

- **Monospace** (code, technical UI): IBM Plex Mono, Roboto Mono, or system monospace
- **Body** (prose, labels): Inter, Helvetica Neue, or system sans-serif

## Visual Identity

**Logo Placeholder:** `assets/logo-placeholder.svg` (256x256 SVG, uppercase "P" in sage green on warm cream background)

**Symbol Rationale:** The letter "P" suggests *page* and *paginate*—the structural unit Paginary reasons about. Sage green (#84B08E) paired with warm cream (#F5F0E8) evokes natural, organic layouts: like pages in a well-designed book. The palette is calming and sophisticated, signaling that content composition is both scientific and aesthetic.

## Brand Voice

Paginary's voice is precise and design-conscious. We speak to developers and designers building adaptive interfaces, emphasizing intelligent layout reasoning and the intersection of AI and visual composition. Copy acknowledges both the technical (model selection, inference) and aesthetic (visual balance, readability) aspects of page generation.
