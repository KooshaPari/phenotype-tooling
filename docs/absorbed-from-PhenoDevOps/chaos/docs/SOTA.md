## Introduction

The hypothesis: "The best way to build confidence in a system's resilience is to subject it to real-world failures in a controlled environment."

## Evolution

### Pre-Chaos Era
Design reviews, testing under nominal conditions.

### Netflix Era (2010-2011)
Chaos Monkey, Simian Army.

### Formalization (2012-2018)
Principles of Chaos Engineering, Pact.

### Mainstream Adoption (2018-Present)
LitmusChaos, Chaos Mesh, Kubernetes-native.
